# Customs edge cases

These are hypothetical review patterns, not actual rulings or verified duty
rates. Each outcome depends on the transaction date, jurisdiction and evidence.

1. **Low-value parcel restructuring.** Do not use the historical $800 rule as a
   current duty-free route. Check CBP's operative entry procedure and restrictions
   for the shipment date. Do not split, undervalue or misidentify importers to
   evade duties. Incoterms/title/control alone do not decide importer status;
   product-agency requirements remain separate from a duty exemption.
2. **Suspected AD/CVD transshipment.** Hold affected decisions for trade counsel
   and preserve production records, BOM, supplier history and exact order scope.
   EAPA addresses AD/CVD evasion; Section301 alone is not an AD/CVD order. Do not
   invent a combined AD/CVD rate from one AD case number, a 30% origin threshold
   or a guarantee that assembly/three changed characteristics settles scope.
3. **EAR/ITAR uncertainty.** Analyse the actual configured item under the correct
   regulations and specially-designed definitions, which are not interchangeable.
   Generic commercial availability, five axes or ±2microns do not independently
   prove an ECCN or USML category. Seek qualified jurisdiction/classification
   review and a formal determination where appropriate. Do not export or release
   controlled technical data pending the necessary clearance; no promised CJ or
   licence turnaround, automatic dual-regime violation or invented exception.
4. **Year-end related-party adjustment.** Map adjustments to affected merchandise
   and examine the original pricing policy, customs acceptability, reconciliation
   status and procedural deadlines. A tax reduction is not automatically a refund
   entitlement, and a reconciliation is not automatically a prior disclosure.
   Present estimated duty effects only after classification/rate verification.
5. **Multi-tier first sale.** Establish a bona fide sale for export, documentary
   chain and arm's-length acceptability, plus actual statutory adjustments. Do
   not equate any buying agent with a reseller, every design with an assist or
   a particular markup with failure. Verify each jurisdiction's rule; do not
   invent a worldwide country whitelist or savings threshold requiring a ruling.
6. **Missed USMCA preference.** Verify origin for each production period and the
   permitted evidence. Under1520(d), calculate one year from importation, not
   liquidation. A generic180-day protest cannot be assumed to rescue an expired
   claim. Separate estimated overpayment from legally recoverable and collected
   amounts. Do not infer a finished assembly's classification from its inputs.
7. **Carnet goods proposed for sale.** Stop unapproved diversion from the admitted
   purpose. Ask customs/broker and the guaranteeing association about authorised
   conversion/discharge or re-export before delivery. Do not assume conversion is
   always forbidden or that filing a retroactive entry fixes everything. Medical
   device marketing/clinical-use approval is independent of duty status.
8. **Coffee kit classification.** Evaluate actual components, domestic/commercial
   appliance characteristics, retail presentation, need/activity and essential
   character. A branded box, highest-value component or separately marketable
   goods are not decisive alone. Classification must precede duty optimisation;
   check food-agency obligations independently. Do not invent codes, rates or a
   future trade agreement to make the preferred answer economical.
9. **False origin marking.** Preserve facts, stop repeating inaccurate declarations
   and seek counsel on origin, marking, applicable additional duty and corrections.
   Section301 duties are distinct from AD/CVD/EAPA. Do not equate invoice value
   with domestic value, add incompatible maximum penalties or promise disclosure
   protection from a single notice-date shortcut. Correct marks and records under
   the authorised procedure, without destroying evidence or inventing culpability.
10. **Forced-labour detention.** Preserve shipment-specific upstream traceability,
    production/payment records and actual notice deadlines. Distinguish a claim
    that goods are outside UFLPA scope from a request to overcome its presumption.
    The presumption includes covered entities, not only geographic inputs; there
    is no general small-percentage exemption. Do not declare an exception legally
    impossible, promise routine extensions, treat a voluntary certification or
    isotope test as conclusive, or automatically divert goods to another market.
    Counsel must assess the permitted response, evidence, reporting and disposal.

## Worked calculation boundaries
