# Building an X Article with a browser agent (optional)

last-verified: 2026-11-13 · the editor changes; if a step stops matching, take a screenshot, adjust, and update this file.

Load only when the author wants the draft built in X's editor by an agent driving their signed-in browser. Everything here ends at a saved, unpublished draft. **Never click Publish.**

## Before starting

- The author's own browser, signed in to X, with their permission for this session. Never their passwords.
- Images ready on disk as JPGs, numbered in article order, the cover already cropped to 5:2.
- The article in HTML (or clean Markdown converted to HTML), split into chunks of one or two sections.

## Order

1. **Title**, typed with real keystrokes.
2. **Cover**, uploaded and applied in the crop dialog.
3. **Body, a chunk at a time.** Pasting HTML keeps paragraphs, subheadings, bold, italic, links and lists. After each chunk, check the last few blocks: two pastes in a row can merge into one paragraph, and a paste right after an image can land short.
4. **Images** at their slots through the editor's insert menu. Count them after each upload.
5. **Code blocks** through the insert menu. Pasted `<pre>` comes in as plain text.
6. **The divider** before the closing offer paragraph.
7. **Captions last.** The caption dialog moves with each image's height; take a screenshot before every click, and confirm the cursor is inside the dialog before typing (stray keystrokes can trigger X's keyboard shortcuts).
8. **Wait for "saved"** in the header before leaving the page.

## Verify, then hand off

- The block order matches the outline: subheadings, lists, images and code blocks all where planned, no empty paragraphs.
- Every link, with the sentence around it, is true and tagged.
- No em dashes, en dashes, [confirm], TODO or TK anywhere in the text.
- The title and cover are set.
- Tell the author what's in the draft, what changed from the doc, and to check it in Preview. They publish.
