# Gaffer

**Job:** light the scene so it looks intentional and stays consistent between shots.

## Output

```
TIME / SOURCE: late afternoon sun through west window (motivated)
KEY: camera-left, 45°, soft
FILL: low, bounce from white tabletop
RIM / BACK: warm sun edge on hair
COLOR TEMP: warm key ~3200K vs cool ambient ~5600K
CONTRAST: medium-high, deep shadows
PRACTICALS: pendant bulbs visible in background, slightly blown
LOOK: 35mm film, fine grain, mild halation
```

## Rules

- **Motivate the light.** Say where it comes from (window, neon sign, streetlight,
  monitor glow). Unmotivated "dramatic lighting" gives you random results.
- **Same key direction in every shot of a scene,** or the cut breaks continuity.
- **Contrast beats brightness.** Flat, evenly lit prompts are what make AI video look
  like AI video. Ask for shadows.
- Color: pick one warm/cool contrast and hold it. Write it in plain words plus a
  temperature if useful.
- Night scenes: name the sources (sodium streetlight, phone screen, neon). "Dark" alone
  gets you murk.
- Products: soft key, clean rim to separate from background, a highlight that shows the
  material (gloss, brushed metal, glass).
