# Google post craft

Load before writing posts in /boring-marketing-google-business-profile Phase 5. Hartline Plumbing, Dallas TX is a sample business; the jobs below are illustrative.

## What a post is for

A post is read by someone already looking at the profile, deciding whether to call. It works when it shows the business doing real work nearby, answers a worry, or gives a real reason to call now. Google hasn't confirmed posts as a ranking factor. Judge them by the view counts in the Posts tab and by calls.

## The four patterns

**Job story.** The strongest post because nobody else can write it.

> Water heater leaking into the ceiling in Garland
>
> A Garland homeowner called Tuesday morning about a brown stain spreading on the hallway ceiling. The 14-year-old gas water heater in the attic had rusted through at the base and the drain pan line was clogged, so the water had nowhere to go. We replaced the tank, cleared the pan line and had hot water back by 3pm. If your water heater lives in the attic, check the pan once a year. A clogged line turns a slow leak into a ceiling repair.
>
> Button: Learn more, to the water heater page.
> Photo brief: the old tank's rusted base or the cleared pan, no house number, no people.

**Seasonal prevention.** Tied to the local calendar, posted before the season, not during it.

> Before the first hard freeze in Dallas
>
> Pipes in exterior walls and attics freeze first. Before the first night in the 20s: disconnect garden hoses, open the cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls, and know where your main shutoff is. If a pipe bursts, shut the main first, then call.
>
> Button: Call now.
> Photo brief: a tech pointing at a main shutoff valve in a garage.

**A real question, answered.** Taken from call notes, texts or reviews, in the customer's words.

> "Is a popping noise from my water heater dangerous?"
>
> Usually not dangerous, but not good either. Popping is water boiling under a layer of sediment at the bottom of the tank. It wastes gas and shortens the tank's life. A flush often fixes it on a tank under about eight years old. Older tanks with heavy sediment are usually closer to replacement.
>
> Button: Learn more, to the water heater page.

**Offer.** Only when real, with terms and an end date in the post.

> $50 off a water heater flush through January 31
>
> Applies to tank water heaters in our service area, one per household, booked by January 31.
>
> Button: Call now. (Set the offer dates in the post so it shows as an offer.)

## Rules

- Point first. The preview shows roughly the first two lines; put the problem and the place there.
- Within the editor's length limit (see gbp-facts.md). Most good posts are 60-150 words.
- No phone number, email or social handle in the text. Use the Call button.
- One action per post, pointing at the page that matches it, with tracking parameters on the link (for example `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp-post`).
- Name one town if the job happened there. Never a list of towns.
- No claims the owner hasn't confirmed (24/7, licensed for gas, warranty terms).
- The business's voice profile applies when it exists.

## Photos

- From the crew's phones. Before-and-after pairs and the failed part are the most useful.
- Screen every photo before upload: faces (unless the person agreed), house numbers and street signs, license plates, paperwork, screens showing customer data.
- Meet the format rules in gbp-facts.md. Google rejects heavily filtered or blurry photos.

## The four-week default

| Week | Pattern | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Job story | The best documented job from the last 30 days |
| 2 | Seasonal prevention | The season starting in the next 2-4 weeks |
| 3 | Real question | The question asked most on calls or in reviews this month |
| 4 | Job story, or an offer if a real one exists | A job in a different town or service from week 1 |

Swap patterns when the business argues for it (a slow season might want two offers) and say why in one line.
