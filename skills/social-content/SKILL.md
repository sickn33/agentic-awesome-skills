## Publication and measurement boundaries

Drafting does not authorize posting, scheduling, paid promotion, tagging or messaging. Verify connected platform/account identity and obtain the authorized action, content and timing before external writes; report publication only after a confirmed result, and reconcile ambiguous sends before retrying. Use licensed media/music, accessibility captions/alt text and permission for customer stories or quotes. Hooks and precise revenue/results must reflect real attributable evidence; do not invent experience, testimonials or emotional anecdotes. Never reuse scraped creator text as your own. Label observational/survivorship and paid-distribution confounding; engagement rate must name its denominator and cannot establish causal revenue impact. Keep personal and company accounts distinct and avoid exporting private messages or unnecessary identifiers to outside tools.
