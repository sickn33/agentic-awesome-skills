# Platform templates (copy-paste scaffolds): judgment lives in SKILL.md; facts in platform-facts.md

Use these scaffolds only after Phase 1 has selected the platform and treatment. Replace every bracketed prompt with material supported by the source, brand context, or approved evidence. Delete any section that does not earn its place; a scaffold is a starting shape, not a reason to force content into a format.

Every scaffold is written to pass `slop-patterns.md`: no thread announcements, no recap posts or slides, no reply-bait closers, no formula hooks. Fill the brackets with the source's own specifics, then lint the result anyway.

For current constraints, posting mechanics, and platform-specific facts, load `platform-facts.md`. For tone, use the matching row in `voice-profile#platform-adaptations`.

## LinkedIn

### Text post: observation to lesson

```text
[Specific observation or tension, with the number or named thing that makes it checkable.]

[Why this matters to the reader in a concrete situation.]

I noticed this when [verifiable situation, customer language, or firsthand detail].

[The lesson or useful distinction.]

[A practical next step, or one question you would actually act on the answer to.]
```

### Text post: contrarian with evidence

```text
[A specific claim a smart peer could dispute, with the number or example that backs it.]

[Evidence or example one, with its context.]

[Evidence or example two.]

[The strongest counterexample, and why it does not change the answer.]

[What to do differently, as one concrete step.]
```

### Carousel

```text
Slide 1: [Specific promise, tension, or useful question]

Slide 2: [Why this matters / the problem underneath it]

Slide 3: [Point one, with a concrete example]

Slide 4: [Point two, with a concrete example]

Slide 5: [Point three, with a concrete example]

Slide 6: [Exception, tradeoff, or common mistake]

Slide 7: [Practical next step the reader can take this week]

Last slide: [The strongest point or one honest closing line. No recap of slides 3-5. At most one ask.]
```

## X

### Single post

```text
[Standalone observation, claim, or useful distinction.]

[One or two lines that make it concrete, with a number or a named detail.]

[Optional: the example or implication that sharpens it. No closing question.]
```

### Thread

```text
Post 1
[The most interesting true thing in the thread, stated plainly with its specific number or detail. Do not announce that a thread follows.]

Post 2
[The scene, data, or evidence behind post 1.]

Post 3 onward
[One point per post, each with its own example, number, or named mechanism.]
[Vary the shape: a one-line post, a two-sentence post, a quoted number. Uniform rhythm reads as template.]

Last post
[The strongest point of the thread, or the one next step. No recap of earlier posts.]
[Optional: one genuine ask or the source link, only if the thread earned it.]
```

## Instagram

### Carousel

```text
Slide 1: [Clear visual hook carrying a specific claim]

Slide 2: [The problem, tension, or promise]

Slide 3: [Point one]

Slide 4: [Point two]

Slide 5: [Point three]

Slide 6: [Example, visual proof, or helpful contrast]

Slide 7: [Practical takeaway]

Last slide: [A checklist or one line the viewer would screenshot. No recap of earlier slides. At most one ask.]
```

### Caption: story

```text
[The most interesting true thing in the story, with one specific detail, as the first line.]

I remember when [personal story setup].

[The struggle or tension.]

Then [turning point].

[Lesson learned or useful shift.]

[Optional: one specific question the reader can answer from their own work or home.]
```

### Caption: list

```text
[Number or named list + concrete benefit.]

1. [Point and brief explanation, with an example]

2. [Point and brief explanation, with an example]

3. [Point and brief explanation, with an example]

[Optional: one specific question that invites a specific response.]
```

### Caption: teach

```text
[Contrarian or surprising statement the source can back up.]

[The explanation, in two or three short paragraphs, using one example from the source.]

What to do instead:
- [Action one]
- [Action two]
- [Action three]

[At most one plain-language ask, or none.]
```

### Reel script

```text
Opening visual: [What the viewer sees immediately]
Spoken hook: [Specific claim, question, or tension]
On-screen text: [Short supporting phrase]

Beat 1: [First idea or demonstration]
Beat 2: [Second idea or demonstration]
Beat 3: [Payoff, example, or contrast]

Close: [Useful next step, loop, or restrained CTA]
```

### Story sequence

```text
Story 1: [Question, tension, or behind-the-scenes opening]
Story 2: [Context or personal detail]
Story 3: [Useful lesson, proof, or demonstration]
Story 4: [A real question whose answers will change what you do next, or the next step. Not a poll whose answer is the pitch.]
```

## TikTok

### Script: rapid teach

```text
Opening frame: [Visual that makes the topic clear]
Spoken hook: [Bold claim or specific promise]

Point 1: [One concise idea]
Point 2: [One concise idea]
Point 3: [One concise idea]

Close: [Takeaway, next step, or natural loop]
```

### Script: story hook

```text
Opening frame: [Start in the middle of the moment]
Spoken hook: [The specific moment, said plainly: when, where, what went wrong or surprised you]

Setup: [Only the context needed]
Tension: [What made this difficult, surprising, or useful]
Payoff: [What happened or what changed]
Lesson: [What the viewer can use]
```

### Script: myth correction

```text
Opening frame: [Visible contrast or proof]
Spoken hook: [The myth in the customer's own words, then the number or example that contradicts it]

Why it is incomplete: [Evidence or explanation]
What is more useful: [The better model]
What to do instead: [Concrete action]
```

### Script: scene

```text
Opening frame: [A moment the viewer has lived, shown rather than announced]

Scene: [Act out the situation, with the specific details that make it recognizable]
Turn: [The unexpected or useful moment]
Close: [Punchline or insight]
```

## YouTube Shorts

### Rapid tip

```text
Hook: [Specific tip or question]
Demonstration: [Show or say the useful action immediately]
Context: [Why it matters]
Close: [Next step or related topic]
```

### Clip

```text
Opening: [Strongest self-contained moment]
Context card or line: [What the viewer needs to know]
Value: [The complete idea, without requiring the longer video]
Close: [Where a viewer can continue if useful]
```

### Transformation

```text
Opening visual: [Outcome or contrast]
Question: [What changed?]
Explanation: [The few steps or decisions that mattered]
Close: [What to try next]
```

### Reaction

```text
Opening: [The item, claim, or question being addressed]
Take: [Your clear point of view]
Reason: [Evidence, example, or caveat]
Close: [The one specific point the original missed, or no close]
```

## Threads

### Conversational take

```text
[Opinion or observation in plain language, with the detail that made you think it.]

[Context: where you saw it, in one line.]

[The insight, including the strongest objection to it.]
```

### Mini-thread

```text
Post 1: [Bold claim or observation with a specific detail]

Post 2: [Supporting point with an example]

Post 3: [Deeper insight or tradeoff, with its own example]

Post 4: [The strongest point or the one next step. No recap.]
```

### Quote-post commentary

```text
[Quote or reshare the source post.]

[Your take in two or three sentences: what the post gets right, and the one thing it leaves out.]
```

## Reddit

### Value post

```text
Title: [Specific, descriptive title]

Body:
[Context: who you are and why you can speak to this]

[Affiliation disclosure, if relevant]

[The actual value: detailed, specific, and useful without a purchase]

[Step-by-step breakdown, example, or evidence]

Edit: [Answer a common question or add useful clarification]
```

### Ask-me-anything post

```text
Title: I am [relevant credentials]. I [relevant, verifiable experience]. Ask me anything about [topic].

Body:
[Brief bio]
[What you can answer questions about]
[Relevant proof or verification, if the community asks for it]

[A clear invitation to ask questions]
```

### Comment

```text
[Answer the question directly.]

[Add a specific example, limitation, or source of experience.]

[Optional follow-up question that helps the original poster.]
```
