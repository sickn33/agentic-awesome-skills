# What actually performs as a Xiaohongshu / X cover

Read at step 2. Method: search the platform for the topic, scroll to load ~100 notes, then sort covers by likes and lay them out as a grid (in the page via JS: `section.note-item` → `a.cover img` + `.count`), screenshot, look.

Top-performing families (AI / indie-dev / side-income niche):
1. Real face or real photo + vlog subtitle (white, thick black outline, yellow keyword). Most common.
2. Text-only card / iOS Notes look: one big sentence on white or pale green.
3. Screenshot of a tweet, a dashboard or revenue with a yellow label or red circle.
4. Phone screenshot zoomed, almost unedited.
5. Minimal tweet-style big text ("8 months. 28 apps. $25K MRR.").
6. Pixel art / game screens.
Almost none look "designed".
