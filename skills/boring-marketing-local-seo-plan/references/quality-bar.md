# The quality bar: what a plan and its handoffs must clear

Load before writing the plan's output and before handing a move to another skill. The bar for everything here: the person doing the work would send it to the owner, or ship it to the site, unedited except for facts only the owner knows. Passing the checklist isn't the bar; that is. Hartline Plumbing, Dallas TX is a sample business, and every number, name slot and position below is illustrative.

## The plan's opening, strong and weak

**Strong.**

> **When someone in Garland searches "water heater repair near me", <competitor A> comes up first and Hartline isn't in the top 20.** That held from every point we checked across Garland and Mesquite, because Hartline's map pin sits on the far side of Dallas from both.
>
> Outside the top 20 in 4 of 5 towns · named in 0 of 6 AI answers · 11 of the last 40 reviews unanswered
>
> **What it's costing you.**
> 1. **Garland and Mesquite go to <competitor A>.** Those are the towns your job records say send the most water heater work outside Dallas. Near-me searches are settled in the map pack, and you aren't in it.
> 2. **One assistant gives your old number under another company's name.** Someone who asks Perplexity for an emergency plumber in Dallas gets your old line, listed as <another business>. Every one of those calls is lost.
> 3. **Unanswered reviews.** 11 of the last 40 have no reply, including three one-star reviews. The next customer reads the silence, and the AI summaries on the profile read the reviews too.
>
> **Fix first.** Change the profile's main category to the water heater category, add the four services you actually do with how you price them, reply to the 11 reviews, and correct the old number on the two directories that still carry it. About 30 minutes of your time. We'll know it worked when the next monthly check shows the right number in the answers and the replies done. Climbing into the Garland map pack is the longer project, because distance from the pin is the one factor no page can change; the Garland page in month two is how the website carries that town meanwhile.

**Weak.**

> Your local SEO score is 42/100. We found 23 issues. Recommendations: optimize your Google Business Profile, build citations, create location pages for all 15 towns in your service area, and improve your content.

Why it fails: a score instead of who's winning, no competitor named, twenty-three findings with no order, "optimize your profile" instead of the exact change, and fifteen town pages the proof can't support.

## The page list, strong and weak

**Strong:** the complete list in build order, every row with a URL, a title tag, a target week and what it rests on (see the table in `decision-examples.md`). Town pages sit on the list with their week and the note "behind the standard".

**Weak:**

> Service pages: to be decided once keyword volumes are pulled. Town pages: on hold until job records are available. Consider location pages for the top towns.

Why it fails: nothing gets built, nobody knows what ships when, and the missing data was a reason to gate pages, not to defer them.

## The handoffs: good versus reject

| Deliverable | Good | Weak (reject) |
|---|---|---|
| The plan or a pitch audit | Leads with who wins instead in plain words; three vitals; three costly gaps with named competitors and evidence; one fix-first move; the complete page list, the profile checklist and 13 dated weeks | A score, jargon, twenty findings, no competitor names, advice that fits any plumber, page decisions deferred behind holds, ledgers padding the plan |
| Service page | Answers the buyer's real questions first (how pricing works, how fast, areas served) with the business's actual services, licenses, reviews and photos; paste-ready copy, title, description and markup | Template filler, invented guarantees or prices, the same page with the town swapped |
| Town page or job story | Real jobs in that town, a local detail that changes the work, reviews from there; clearly different from its siblings | A doorway page: the town name swapped, no proof |
| Review replies | Specific to what the reviewer said, in the owner's voice; calm with a next step on a bad review | "Thanks for the review!" on every one; defensive replies; stuffed keywords |
| Profile fix list | Exact changes in order (this category to that one, these services at these prices, this description), about 20 minutes in one sitting | "Optimize your Google Business Profile" |
| List placement plan | The specific lists the AI answers cite, who runs each, what they require, a ready pitch | "Get more backlinks", guessed contacts |
| Monthly update | Move by move: what we did, what changed with before and after from the same check, what's next, and the moves that didn't work | A dashboard dump, vanity totals, misses left out |

## Monthly update, strong

> **Last month's moves.** Foundations: done (category, services, 11 replies, old number fixed on 2 of 2 directories). Garland page: published on the 12th, indexed on the 15th.
> **What changed.** Garland near-me from the town center: outside the top 20 to #14 (same point, same phone). Perplexity now gives the right number. Named in 1 of 6 AI answers, up from 0. Dallas didn't move.
> **What didn't work.** The list editor for "best plumbers in Dallas" hasn't replied after two follow-ups; moving that effort to the second list.
> **Next.** Money cell: drains in Dallas (sitting at #5). Slab leak page improvements.

Every before-and-after comes from the same query, place and device as the baseline. A change measured a different way is a new baseline, not progress.
