# What to take from each reference

## Spotify Wrapped 2025 (60fps.design screen recordings; Spotify design Q&A)
- Patterns (stripes, polka dots, concentric rings, wave bands) ARE the transitions; they scale up to fill the frame and hug content edges.
- One thin hand-drawn line threads screen to screen, forming stars, circling numbers.
- Extreme type scale contrast: huge stretched display numerals vs tiny clean text.
- Photos framed by pattern borders (stamp edges, stripe frames) become graphic objects.
- Black / off-white base, one accent colour per moment; full-pattern screens alternate with airy ones.

## Figma Config openers (Relay, Copenhagen; Figma brand blog)
- 2025: frame rate dropped 60 → 15 fps for a tactile, handmade feel.
- 2026: glyphs and textures thread, stack, loop and break out of containers — "looser, more collage-y".
- Static design and motion developed together; dedicated sound design.

## Ordinary Folk — Gemini app launch (2D UI MG)
- Soft pastel gradient fields, one centred line with a single highlighted word, UI crops, floating phones, dark section for contrast, glowing ring for the climax.

## OpenAI — ChatGPT Pulse launch
- White canvas, small centred type, huge whitespace, a plain white phone, beautiful illustrations as the hero, UI chips animating in.

## Linear / Cursor / Raycast (Impractical breakdowns)
- Masked text reveals from invisible edges (no blur/scale fades); sharp acceleration + long deceleration, no bounce; generous holds.
- Hard cuts are fine when framing changes; aggressive punch-ins on the moment of value.
- Raycast: withhold information, scramble text, audio drop-outs, a zero-ease punch-in finale.

## BUCK × Notion AI; Duolingo (Rive)
- Hand-drawn characters animated in cel for handmade feel; personality from the timing of a blink.
- Story over feature list; every cut on an audio beat; type integrated with character motion.

## Category / competitor App Store art (photo-trend apps)
- dooo0t: Y2K glitter, scribbled stars/hearts, doodle outline around people, stamp-edge frames.
- WalkColor: italic serif + highlighter blocks, dot-grid paper, half-split punch-hole swaps, offset colour blocks behind tilted phones.
- DotArt: bold half-and-half compositions (stripes + notes, polka holes revealing a face).
- Their photos: flash, film, candid Gen-Z subjects = "网感".

## Code-motion effect libraries (ideas, re-implemented in canvas)
- React Bits: SplitFlapText, RotatingText, CircularText / CurvedLoop, StickerPeel, HalftoneReveal, DotGrid, MetaBalls, CircularGallery / DomeGallery, FlyingPosters, PixelTransition, ImageTrail, Shuffle.

## Getting frames when browsing is blocked
- The in-app browser may be refused on YouTube / Vimeo and trigger per-site permission prompts; use `scripts/ref_frames.sh` (yt-dlp + ffmpeg) instead. Behance blocks curl; 60fps.design pages expose direct mp4 URLs.
